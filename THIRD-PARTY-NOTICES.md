# Dipendenze

La distribuzione include .NET/WPF (Microsoft), Microsoft.Data.Sqlite.Core, SQLitePCLRaw/SourceGear.sqlite3, ClosedXML e le loro dipendenze. Le versioni risolte sono nei `packages.lock.json` della repository.

- .NET: https://github.com/dotnet/runtime/blob/main/LICENSE.TXT
- WPF: https://github.com/dotnet/wpf/blob/main/LICENSE.TXT
- Microsoft.Data.Sqlite: https://github.com/dotnet/efcore/blob/main/LICENSE.txt
- SQLitePCLRaw: https://github.com/ericsink/SQLitePCL.raw/blob/master/LICENSE.TXT
- SQLite: https://sqlite.org/copyright.html
- ClosedXML: https://github.com/ClosedXML/ClosedXML/blob/develop/LICENSE
- Carlito (font metrico incorporato da ClosedXML): https://github.com/ClosedXML/ClosedXML/tree/develop/ClosedXML/Graphics/Fonts

I file Excel e i dati bancari personali non fanno parte della distribuzione.
